> **Standard Taxonomy:** `#econometrics`, `#survey_design`, `#data_source`, `#data_engineering`, `#coding_architecture`, `#visualization`, `#literature_review`, `#ai`, `#ai_agents`, `#skills`, `#spatial_analysis`, `#impact_evaluation`, `#economics`, `#labor_economics`, `#system_dynamics`, `#industrial_economics`, `#growth`

For full course-note-style write-ups (math, derivations, step-by-step procedure) that outgrow a single entry here, create a dedicated `methodology_<topic>.md` in this folder — see `_examples/` for the expected depth.
